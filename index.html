IGCSE Biology U10-13总结

Unit 10: Diseases and Immunity (疾病与免疫) 1. Pathogens & Transmission (病原体与传播) • Pathogen (病原体): A microorganism that causes disease. (引起疾病的微生物) • Transmissible Disease (可传播疾病): Can be passed from host to host. (可在宿主间传播的疾病) • Transmission (传播): Movement of pathogen from one host to another. (病原体从一个宿主到另一个的移动) • Direct Contact (直接接触): e.g., Blood-to-blood (HIV - 艾滋病病毒), shared towel (Athlete's foot - 足癣). • Indirect Transmission (间接传播): • Airborne/Droplets (空气/飞沫): Coughs, sneezes (Cold, Flu, COVID-19 - 感冒、流感、新冠病毒). • Contaminated Food/Water (受污染的食物/水): (Salmonella - 沙门氏菌, Cholera - 霍乱). • Surface Contact (接触表面): Touching contaminated surfaces then mouth. • 难点/考点 (Difficulty/Exam Point): Clearly distinguishing between direct contact and the various indirect transmission methods. You must be able to list examples. (清晰区分直接接触和各种间接传播方式，并能够举例说明。) 2. Body Defenses (身体防御) • First Line of Defence (第一道防线): • Skin (皮肤): Physical barrier. (物理屏障) • Mucus & Cilia (黏液与纤毛): In airways, trap and move pathogens up and out. (在呼吸道中，捕捉并向上移动病原体排出) • Stomach Acid (胃酸): Kills pathogens. (杀灭病原体) • Tears/Saliva (眼泪/唾液): Contain lysozyme (溶菌酶) that kills bacteria. • Urine Flow (尿流): Flushes pathogens from bladder/urethra. (将病原体冲出膀胱/尿道) • Second Line of Defence (第二道防线 - White Blood Cells 白细胞): • Phagocytosis (吞噬作用): Phagocytes (吞噬细胞) engulf and digest pathogens. (吞噬细胞包围并消化病原体) • Antibody Production (抗体产生): Lymphocytes (淋巴细胞) produce antibodies (抗体) to destroy pathogens. • 难点/考点 (Difficulty/Exam Point): Understanding the specificity of antibodies. Each antibody has a specific shape complementary to an antigen (抗原) on a pathogen. (理解抗体的特异性：每种抗体都有一个与病原体上抗原互补的特定形状。) 3. The Immune Response & Immunity (免疫反应与免疫力) • Antigen (抗原): Molecules on pathogen's surface. (病原体表面的分子) • Antibody (抗体): Protein molecules produced by lymphocytes; shape fits antigens. (淋巴细胞产生的蛋白质分子，形状与抗原契合) • Memory Cells (记忆细胞): Long-lived lymphocytes that remain after infection. It allows a faster, stronger secondary immune response, preventing illness. (感染后长期存活的淋巴细胞，允许更快、更强的二次免疫反应，防止生病。) • Immunity Types (免疫力类型): Feature Active Immunity (主动免疫) Passive Immunity (被动免疫) Source (来源) Body's own lymphocytes make antibodies & memory cells (自身产生抗体和记忆细胞) Antibodies from another organism (从外部获得抗体) How Acquired (获取方式) 1. Catching & recovering from disease (自然感染) 2. Vaccination (疫苗接种) 1. Mother’s breast milk (母乳) 2. Injections of antibodies (注射抗体血清) Duration (持久性) Long-term (Often lifelong because memory cells exist) (长期，常有终生免疫) Short-term (No memory cells, antibodies break down) (短期，无记忆细胞) • 难点/考点 (Difficulty/Exam Point): Vaccination (疫苗接种) works by introducing a weakened/dead pathogen or its antigens. This triggers your immune system to produce memory cells without getting sick. Understand why the primary response is slow, and the secondary response is fast & massive. (疫苗接种的原理：它引入弱化/死亡的病原体或其抗原，在不生病的前提下触发免疫系统产生记忆细胞。必须理解初次免疫反应慢，二次免疫反应又快又强的原因。) 4. Cholera Case Study (霍乱案例研究) • Pathogen: Bacterium (细菌) • Transmission: Contaminated water/food with faeces. (被粪便污染的水/食物) • Mechanism (机制): 1. Bacteria produce a toxin (毒素) in small intestine. 2. Toxin stimulates cells to secrete chloride ions (Cl⁻) into the lumen. 3. This lowers the water potential (水势) in the lumen. 4. Osmosis (渗透作用): Water moves from blood into the intestine. • Result: Severe diarrhoea (腹泻) -> dehydration (脱水) and loss of ions; can be fatal. • 难点/考点 (Difficulty/Exam Point): Tracing the chain of events from the cholera toxin action to the symptom of diarrhoea. A classic FRQ topic. (追踪从霍乱毒素作用到腹泻症状的完整链条。这是一个经典的FRQ主题。) --- Unit 11: Respiration and Gas Exchange (呼吸与气体交换) 1. Respiration (呼吸作用) • Definition: The process of releasing energy from glucose. (从葡萄糖中释放能量的过程) • Aerobic Respiration (有氧呼吸): • Equation: Glucose + Oxygen -> Carbon Dioxide + Water + Energy (大量) • Location: Mitochondria (线粒体) • Anaerobic Respiration (无氧呼吸): • In Humans (人体): Glucose -> Lactic Acid + Energy (少量). (产生乳酸) • In Plants/Yeast (植物/酵母): Glucose -> Ethanol + Carbon Dioxide + Energy. (产生酒精和二氧化碳) • Oxygen Debt (氧债): The amount of oxygen needed to break down the lactic acid built up during anaerobic respiration. (分解无氧呼吸积累的乳酸所需的氧量。) 2. Gas Exchange in Humans (人体气体交换) • Alveoli (肺泡): Site of gas exchange. (气体交换的场所) • Adaptations of Alveoli (肺泡的适应性特征): 1. Large surface area (大表面积): Total area > 100 m². 2. Thin walls (壁薄): One cell thick for short diffusion distance. 3. Good blood supply (丰富血供): Maintains concentration gradient. 4. Ventilation (通气): Breathing movements keep air fresh. • Inspired vs Expired Air (吸入气 vs 呼出气): Gas Inspired (吸入) Expired (呼出) Oxygen (O₂) High (21%) Low (16%) Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) Low (0.04%) High (4%) Water Vapour (水蒸气) Variable Saturated (饱和) • 难点/考点 (Difficulty/Exam Point): Explaining why expired air has more CO₂ and less O₂. Using the concepts of diffusion and concentration gradients between the alveoli and blood. You must be able to link the structure of alveoli to their function for FRQs. (解释为什么呼出气有更多CO₂和更少O₂。用扩散和浓度梯度的概念联系肺泡与血液。对于FRQ，必须能将肺泡的结构与其功能联系起来。) --- Unit 12: Coordination and Response (协调与反应) 1. The Nervous System (神经系统) • Neurone (神经元): A nerve cell adapted to carry electrical signals. • Parts of a Neurone (神经元结构): Dendrite (树突) -> Cell Body (胞体) -> Axon (轴突) -> Nerve Endings (神经末梢). • Myelin Sheath (髓鞘): Insulates axon, speeds up impulse conduction. • Synapse (突触): A gap between neurones. • Mechanism: Electrical impulse arrives -> releases neurotransmitter (神经递质) -> diffuses across gap -> binds to receptor on next neurone -> triggers new impulse. • Reflex Arc (反射弧): • Stimulus (刺激) -> Receptor (感受器) -> Sensory Neurone (感觉神经元) -> Relay Neurone (中间神经元) (in CNS) -> Motor Neurone (运动神经元) -> Effector (效应器) -> Response (反应). • Feature: Automatic (自动的), rapid (快速的), involuntary (不随意的). Protects body from harm. 2. Hormones (激素) - The Endocrine System (内分泌系统) • Definition: Chemical messengers secreted by glands (腺体), transported in blood (血液), target specific target organs (靶器官) to cause a slow, long-lasting effect. • Fight or Flight Response (战或逃反应): • Adrenaline (肾上腺素) from adrenal glands (肾上腺). • Effects: Increase heart rate (心率加快), breathing rate (呼吸加快), pupil dilation (瞳孔放大), release glucose from liver (肝脏释放葡萄糖). 3. Coordination in Plants (植物的协调作用) - Tropisms (向性) • Tropism (向性): A growth (生长) response to a stimulus (stimuli). • Auxin (生长素): A plant hormone that controls growth. • Phototropism (向光性): • Shoot (茎): Positive (positive - 向光性). Auxin concentrates on shady side (背光侧), causing cells to elongate faster, bending towards light. • Gravitropism (Geotropism)(向地性): • Root (根): Positive (towards gravity - 正向地性). Auxin on lower side inhibits (抑制) cell elongation, causing root to bend down. • Shoot (茎): Negative (away from gravity - 负向地性). Auxin on lower side promotes (促进) cell elongation, causing shoot to bend up. • 难点/考点 (Difficulty/Exam Point): Comparing and contrasting the nervous system (electrical, fast, short-lived) vs. the endocrine system (chemical, slow, long-lasting). Also, understanding the uneven distribution of auxin (生长素分布不均) and its opposite (不同) effect on roots vs. shoots for tropism questions. (FRQ中常问：神经系统与内分泌系统的对比。以及理解生长素分布不均及其对根和茎的相反作用。) --- Unit 13: Excretion and Homeostasis (排泄与稳态) 1. Excretion & Urea (排泄与尿素) • Excretion (排泄): Removal of metabolic wastes (代谢废物) from the body. (e.g., CO₂ from lungs, urea from kidneys) • Urea Formation (尿素的形成): • Location: Liver (肝脏) • Process: Deamination (脱氨基作用) • Excess amino acids cannot be stored. • Liver removes the nitrogen-containing part (含氮部分) and converts it to urea (尿素). • The rest (energy-containing) is converted to carbohydrate (碳水化合物) for storage. • Excretion of Urea (尿素的排泄): • Urea is toxic. • Transported in blood plasma to kidneys. • Kidneys (肾脏) filter urea out -> produce urine (尿液). • Urine Path (尿路): Kidney (肾) -> Ureter (输尿管) -> Bladder (膀胱) -> Urethra (尿道) -> Out. 2. Structure of the Kidney & Nephron (肾脏与肾单位的结构) • Kidney Structure: Outer Cortex (皮质), Inner Medulla (髓质). Filled with thousands of nephrons (肾单位). • Nephron (肾单位): The functional unit of the kidney. • Glomerulus (肾小球): A knot of capillaries. Ultrafiltration (超滤) occurs here: High blood pressure forces water, urea, glucose, ions out of the blood into the kidney tubule. Large molecules (proteins, blood cells) stay in blood. • Proximal Convoluted Tubule (近曲小管): Reabsorption (重吸收) of useful substances. All glucose (全部葡萄糖) and most water/ions are reabsorbed into blood by active transport (主动运输). • Loop of Henle (髓袢): Creates a high salt concentration in the medulla, needed for water reabsorption. • Collecting Duct (集合管): Final adjustment of water content. Reabsorbs water if the body is dehydrated. • 难点/考点 (Difficulty/Exam Point): The role of Active Transport (主动运输) in reabsorbing glucose. Explaining why glucose appears in the urine of a diabetic person (cannot fully reabsorb due to high concentration in filtrate - 滤液中浓度过高无法完全重吸收). The function of Loop of Henle (髓袢) for creating concentration gradient. 3. Homeostasis (稳态) - Blood Glucose Regulation (血糖调节) • Homeostasis (稳态): Maintaining a constant internal environment (内部环境) despite external changes. • Blood Glucose Level (血糖水平): • Controlled by negative feedback (负反馈). • When High (After eating 进食后): 1. Pancreas (胰腺) detects high glucose. 2. Secretes Insulin (胰岛素). 3. Liver converts glucose to glycogen (糖原). 4. Blood glucose level falls. • When Low (After exercise/exercise 运动后): 5. Pancreas detects low glucose. 2. Secretes Glucagon (胰高血糖素). 3. Liver converts glycogen (糖原) to glucose (葡萄糖). 4. Blood glucose level rises. • Diabetes Mellitus (糖尿病): • Type 1: Body doesn't produce enough insulin. Must be treated with insulin injections (注射胰岛素). Cannot be taken orally (a protein, would be digested). • Type 2: Body cells don't respond to insulin. Often linked to diet. • 难点/考点 (Difficulty/Exam Point): Negative feedback (负反馈) is the key concept. You must be able to describe the complete loop: Stimulus → Receptor → Coordinator → Effector → Response → Returns to normal. Must also be able to explain why insulin injections (注射) are needed for Type 1 Diabetes and why sensor monitoring (传感器监测) is better than dipsticks for managing it. (负反馈是关键概念，必须能完整描述闭环。同时需解释I型糖尿病需要注射胰岛素的原因，以及为什么传感器比试纸更优。)

// 标签切换功能实现
document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', function() {
    const tabs = document.querySelectorAll('.tab-btn');
    const contents = document.querySelectorAll('.tab-content');
    
    tabs.forEach(tab => {
        tab.addEventListener('click', () => {
            // 移除所有激活状态
            tabs.forEach(t => t.classList.remove('active'));
            contents.forEach(c => c.classList.remove('active'));
            
            // 激活当前标签
            tab.classList.add('active');
            const tabId = tab.getAttribute('data-tab');
            document.getElementById(tabId).classList.add('active');
        });
    });
    
    // 初始化代码高亮
    hljs.highlightAll();
});
